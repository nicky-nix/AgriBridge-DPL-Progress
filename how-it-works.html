How It Works

Simple for sellers, buyers, and drivers

Surplus produce goes from listing to doorstep in a few steps, often the same day.

  1. Create your accountRegister as a farmer, cooperative, trader, or distributor. Our team verifies your basic business details and shows you how to list.
  2. List your surplusAdd the product, quantity, condition, location, original price, a discounted price, and how long it will keep. Photos help it sell.
  3. Get matched with nearby buyersYour listing is shown to restaurants, carinderias, groceries, and food processors close to you.
  4. Confirm the orderWhen a buyer orders, confirm availability and agree on the final price and quantity through the platform.
  5. Hand over and recover valuePrepare the produce for pickup or delivery. The sale is recorded, and income comes in from produce that might have been lost.
  1. Create your accountRegister your business: restaurant, carinderia, grocery, market stall, hotel kitchen, or food processor.
  2. Browse and filterSearch by product, category, distance, price, and time left. Sort by what expires soonest or the biggest discount.
  3. Compare listingsCheck condition, quantity, supplier, and delivery time side by side to find the best fit.
  4. Order or message the supplierPlace an order, or send an inquiry to ask questions and agree on price and quantity.
  5. Pickup or deliveryCollect the produce yourself or request local delivery, often the same day.
  6. Rate or reportTell us how it went. Buyers can report inaccurate listings or poor quality, which keeps the marketplace trustworthy.

Questions

Frequently asked

What counts as surplus or near-expiry produce?

Produce that is still safe and good to use but may not sell before it spoils, such as ripe fruit, a harvest bigger than expected, or items close to their best-by time. Every listing states its condition and how long it will keep.

How do I know the produce is good?

Sellers must describe the condition accurately, and listings show quantity, condition, and time left. Buyers can report inaccurate listings or poor quality, and our team follows up.

How does payment work?

In the pilot, buyer and supplier confirm price and quantity on the platform and arrange pickup or delivery together. Payment options will be confirmed as the pilot begins.

Do I need a cold storage or my own truck?

No. We work with existing tricycles, haulers, and couriers for short, same-day trips, so neither sellers nor AgriBridge need cold storage or a vehicle fleet.

Is AI already making the price suggestions?

Our pilot starts with simple discount rules and manual matching. Smarter demand forecasting and dynamic pricing are planned as the marketplace collects real data.

Where is AgriBridge available?

We are starting in Dipolog City and nearby municipalities in Zamboanga del Norte, then growing to Region IX.
