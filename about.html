About Us

A student-built bridge between surplus and demand.

AgriBridge DPL is a student-led initiative from St. Vincent's College Incorporated, built to keep good produce from going to waste in Zamboanga del Norte.

Our story

One paradox, one local fix

The Philippines depends on agriculture, yet a large share of what farmers grow is lost before it reaches a customer. We saw that gap from our own corner of the country: farmers with produce they could not sell in time, and small food businesses squeezed by rising ingredient prices, often only a few kilometers apart.

AgriBridge DPL began as a student project for the Philippine Startup Challenge XI. Our idea is simple: give suppliers a place to list surplus and near-expiry produce, show it to nearby businesses at a fair discount, and move it quickly with the tricycles, haulers, and couriers that already serve the area.

We are starting small, in Dipolog City, so we can learn from real farmers and real buyers before growing across Zamboanga del Norte and Region IX.

Our mission

To stabilize the local food corridor by connecting suppliers with nearby businesses before produce loses its value, so suppliers recover income, buyers save money, and less food is wasted.

The food-waste problem

Strong harvests, heavy losses

Agriculture is a pillar of the economy, but poor handling, limited post-harvest facilities, and supply-chain gaps mean much of the harvest never gets sold.

9.1%

of the country's GDP came from agriculture, forestry, and fishing in 2024.

20–40%

post-harvest losses reported by the Department of Agriculture for high-value crops.

Up to 50%

lost along the value chain, from harvesting and grading to transport, storage, and distribution.

Sources: Philippine Statistics Authority; Department of Agriculture; a study of food loss in the Philippine agricultural value chain.

How AgriBridge reduces it

See surplus early

As the marketplace collects sales and demand data, forecasting tools will help suppliers spot likely oversupply before produce spoils.

Match with nearby buyers

Surplus is listed at a discount and shown to restaurants, carinderias, groceries, and food processors close by.

Move it within hours

We coordinate existing tricycles, haulers, and couriers for same-day delivery, so no cold chain is needed.

Surplus→Listed→Matched→Delivered→Waste avoided
