How it works

From surplus to sale in three steps

See the full process
1

Sellers list surplus

Farmers, cooperatives, and distributors post what they have, its condition, and how long it will keep.

2

Buyers find a deal

Nearby restaurants, carinderias, and groceries browse discounted produce and place an order.

3

Local delivery, fast

Tricycles, haulers, and couriers move it the same day, with no cold storage needed.
